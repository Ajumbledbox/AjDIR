Ajumbledbox Data Integrity Retention

Intent:
Attempt to limit what remains of our digital footprint on our devices, of our personal information and our personal items,
while at the same time providing a clearer avenue of recovery or usage of one's information,
either for oneself or for others, in those unexpected times when you have to recover your information.

The included book:
Personal_Information_Control_Final_Book.pdf
gives a basic understanding. You decide what is included and how.
